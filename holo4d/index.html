Overview

Reconstructing a dynamic 4D scene along a novel camera trajectory requires both accurate geometry from the source video and generative completion of disoccluded target-view regions. Feed-forward 4D reconstructors recover increasingly comprehensive input-view geometry, but remain tied to the observed camera stream. Camera-controlled video diffusion models provide strong generative priors, yet their camera control is typically not precise enough for metric novel-view reconstruction.

Holo4D connects these capabilities with a hybrid 4D geometric cache built from the source video. Static content is fused from camera-covering source views, dynamic regions remain time-synchronized, and dense 3D tracks provide persistent motion cues. Together with target-camera rays and explicit validity masks, these signals guide a pretrained video diffusion model to synthesize target-view RGB video.

Framework

The source video is reconstructed once. Holo4D reprojects its static geometry and dynamic motion into the target camera, then uses the resulting scaffold to control video diffusion.
